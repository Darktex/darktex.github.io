Differential privacy on PyTorch

PyTorch Developer Day · November 2020

An eleven-minute introduction to Opacus, the PyTorch library for training with differential privacy.

The part I am proudest of is the animation. A mosaic of small CIFAR-10 images, a few pixels each, assembles into the Mona Lisa when you zoom out. Then each tile gets noise, the way differential privacy adds noise to each person’s contribution: every individual image turns to a blur, and the Mona Lisa is still there. That is the promise of the method in one picture. You learn the population and you cannot recover the individual.
