I train and post-train large language models, and build the infrastructure that makes reinforcement learning on them work. I led post-training for Llama 3.0 and the team behind Llama Guard, and co-created OpenEnv, an open standard for RL environments.

I write here for ML engineers who know supervised learning well and want the rest of the map.
